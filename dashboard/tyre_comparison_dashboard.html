Scope boundaries Section 0 — these do not move
1. The trigger is always manual. A person runs the pipeline for a chosen company and filing. One click can run all nine companies for a quarter — that is still a person pressing a button. There is no cron, no polling, no watcher and no unattended re-run anywhere in this tool.
2. No unattended storage of scraped source documents. Retrieved filing text stays in runs/<run-id>/ as working space for that run. What leaves the run is reviewed output: the records you approve here and the workbook you export. Nothing is archived or synced on a schedule.
