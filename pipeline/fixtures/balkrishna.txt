MANAGEMENT COMMENTARY
Volumes for the quarter were 82,412 tonnes, up 6.8% year on year, with agricultural demand
in Europe recovering from a weak base and the earthmover segment holding steady. Natural
rubber remained the largest single cost line and averaged higher than the March quarter;
crude-derivative inputs, principally carbon black and synthetic rubber, also increased,
though the Company's carbon black backward integration at Bhuj absorbed part of the impact.
Realisations were broadly stable, and the Company does not currently intend to pass through
input costs in the export replacement channel while it continues to build market share.

Capital expenditure for the quarter was INR 412.66 Crore, mainly on the Bhuj capacity
expansion and on the second phase of the carbon black plant. Capital expenditure guidance
for FY26 remains at INR 1,300 Crore to INR 1,500 Crore, unchanged from the guidance given
in May 2025, and continues to be funded from internal accruals.

The unaudited financial results were reviewed by the Audit Committee and approved by the
Board of Directors at its meeting held on 5 August 2025.
