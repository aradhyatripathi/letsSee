SEGMENT INFORMATION
The Company operates in a single reportable segment, being the lease of its tyre
manufacturing facility. No channel-wise or product-category-wise breakdown of revenue is
applicable or reported.

MANAGEMENT COMMENTARY
Lease rental income for the quarter was in line with the contracted schedule and the
facility operated without interruption. The Company's revenue is contractual and is not
directly exposed to movements in natural rubber prices or in crude-derivative inputs such
as carbon black and synthetic rubber; those costs are borne by the lessee, although a
sustained increase in them could affect the lessee's utilisation of the facility over the
medium term.

Capital expenditure of Rs. 0.95 Crore during the quarter related to statutory upgrades at
the leased facility. No material capital expenditure programme is planned for the remainder
of FY26; surplus cash generated continues to be deployed in debt mutual funds and fixed
deposits pending a decision on the healthcare business investment announced earlier.
