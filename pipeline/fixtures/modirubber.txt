MANAGEMENT COMMENTARY
Revenue for the quarter came principally from the tyre trading and distribution business
and from rental income on the Company's surplus properties. Trading volumes were broadly in
line with the March quarter. Procurement costs for traded tyres rose during the quarter,
reflecting the pass-through of higher natural rubber prices and of crude-derivative inputs
such as carbon black and synthetic rubber by the manufacturers from whom the Company
sources, and this restricted the trading margin.

Capital expenditure during the quarter was limited to Rs. 1.02 Crore on the refurbishment
of the Modipuram premises. The Company has no manufacturing capital expenditure programme
for FY26 and continues to evaluate options for the monetisation of its non-core land
holdings.

The unaudited financial results were reviewed by the Audit Committee and approved by the
Board of Directors at its meeting held on 13 August 2025. The statutory auditors have
carried out a limited review of these results.
