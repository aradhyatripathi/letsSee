MANAGEMENT COMMENTARY
Volume growth in the domestic replacement channel remained steady through the quarter,
while original equipment offtake was softer on the back of a slower commercial vehicle
build schedule. Natural rubber prices stayed elevated for most of the quarter following
constrained supply from South East Asia, and crude-derivative inputs — carbon black and
synthetic rubber in particular — moved up sequentially, together compressing gross margin
by roughly 90 basis points against the March quarter. The Company expects raw material
costs to remain firm in the September quarter and has taken calibrated price increases in
the replacement channel from July 2025.

Capital expenditure for the quarter was INR 402.18 Crore, directed mainly at the passenger
car radial capacity expansion at Andhra Pradesh and at automation projects across existing
plants. The Board has reaffirmed the full-year capital expenditure guidance of INR 1,700
Crore to INR 1,900 Crore for FY26, funded entirely from internal accruals.

The above results were reviewed by the Audit Committee and approved by the Board of
Directors at its meeting held on 8 August 2025. The statutory auditors have carried out a
