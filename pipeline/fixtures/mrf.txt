MANAGEMENT DISCUSSION — QUARTER ENDED 30TH JUNE 2025
Demand in the replacement market held up well across truck and passenger categories, and
the Company maintained realisations without resorting to discounting. Input costs were the
principal drag on the quarter: domestic natural rubber traded at a premium to imported
grades through most of the period, and crude-linked inputs including synthetic rubber and
carbon black rose sequentially, taking the raw material basket up by close to 4% quarter on
quarter. The Company expects natural rubber prices to ease modestly with the onset of the
peak tapping season, though crude-derivative costs are likely to stay firm.

Capital expenditure during the quarter was Rs. 588.20 Crore, primarily towards capacity
debottlenecking at the Tiruchirappalli and Medak plants and towards the ongoing two-wheeler
radial expansion. The Company continues to fund its capital expenditure programme from
internal accruals and expects full-year spend of approximately Rs. 2,200 Crore.
