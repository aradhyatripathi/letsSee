MANAGEMENT COMMENTARY
Revenue grew 8.1% year on year, led by the two-wheeler and passenger car replacement
channels and by continued traction in the international business. Margin performance
improved sequentially but remains below the year-ago quarter: natural rubber costs stayed
above INR 200 per kg through the quarter, and crude-derivative inputs such as carbon black,
synthetic rubber and rubber chemicals were higher sequentially. Pricing actions taken in
the replacement channel in April and June 2025 have partly offset the input cost increase,
and the Company expects a further improvement in margin in the second quarter if raw
material prices stabilise at current levels.

Capital expenditure of INR 236.55 Crore in the quarter was directed at the Chennai
passenger car radial expansion and at the off-highway tyre capacity at Ambernath. Planned
capital expenditure for FY26 is INR 1,000 Crore, weighted towards the second half of the
year, and will be funded through internal accruals with no incremental long-term borrowing
contemplated.
