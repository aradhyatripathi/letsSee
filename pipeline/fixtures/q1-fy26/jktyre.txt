MANAGEMENT COMMENTARY
Truck radialisation continues to support volume in the replacement channel, and the
Company's Mexican subsidiary delivered a stable quarter despite softer North American
freight activity. Gross margin was 120 basis points lower than the corresponding quarter of
the previous year, driven by natural rubber, which averaged materially above the year-ago
level, and by crude-derivative inputs — carbon black, synthetic rubber and process oils —
which rose sequentially. A price increase of about 1.5% was implemented in the replacement
channel in June 2025, with the benefit expected largely in the second quarter.

Deleveraging remains a priority: gross borrowings reduced by Rs. 162.30 Crore during the
quarter. Capital expenditure of Rs. 188.44 Crore was spent principally on the truck radial
capacity expansion at Banmore and on debottlenecking at Chennai. The Board has approved
capital expenditure of up to Rs. 750 Crore for FY26, to be funded from internal accruals
and existing sanctioned facilities.

The results were reviewed by the Audit Committee and approved by the Board of Directors at
its meeting held on 6th August, 2025. The statutory auditors have issued an unmodified
limited review conclusion.
