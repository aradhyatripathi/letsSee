MANAGEMENT COMMENTARY
Two-wheeler original equipment demand was healthy through the quarter on the back of a
strong scooter build schedule, while the replacement channel grew in line with the market.
Premium radial tyres continued to increase as a share of the two-wheeler mix, supporting
realisations. On costs, natural rubber prices remained elevated for the third consecutive
quarter and crude-derivative inputs, particularly synthetic rubber and carbon black, were
higher sequentially; together these held the EBITDA margin close to the March quarter level
despite operating leverage from higher volume.

Capital expenditure for the quarter was Rs. 52.10 Crore, largely towards the two-wheeler
radial capacity addition at Madurai and towards the new mixing line. The Company expects
FY26 capital expenditure of approximately Rs. 220 Crore, funded from internal accruals and
existing working-capital facilities.
