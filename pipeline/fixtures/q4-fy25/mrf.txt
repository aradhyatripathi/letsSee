MANAGEMENT DISCUSSION — QUARTER ENDED 31ST MARCH 2025
Volumes in the March quarter were led by truck and bus radials, where a firmer freight
cycle and pre-buying ahead of the new financial year lifted replacement offtake, while
original equipment supplies grew on the back of a strong passenger vehicle build
schedule at the Company's principal customers. Realisations improved marginally on the
price revision taken in February, which offset part of the cost pressure carried into
the quarter. The raw material basket eased by roughly 2% sequentially as domestic
natural rubber came off its December highs with improved arrivals and as carbon black
and synthetic rubber contracts settled lower on softer crude; the Company expects the
benefit of the cheaper basket to be more visible in the first half of the coming year,
although natural rubber remains the variable to watch once the lean tapping months
begin.

Capital expenditure during the quarter was Rs. 612.80 Crore, directed mainly at the new
passenger car radial line at Perambalur, mixing and curing capacity at Puducherry and
the completion of the captive solar and wind additions commissioned in March. Full-year
capital expenditure was funded entirely from internal accruals and borrowings were held
broadly flat over the year.
