MANAGEMENT COMMENTARY
The March quarter closed the financial year with firm demand in the domestic replacement
channel, where farm and passenger vehicle fitments both added volume, while original
equipment offtake was broadly flat as commercial vehicle build rates stayed range-bound
through the quarter. Natural rubber prices eased from their December peak as Thai and
Indonesian supply normalised, but carbon black and synthetic rubber held above year-ago
levels, so the raw material basket was only marginally lighter sequentially and gross
margin finished about 40 basis points below the December quarter. The Company carried a
full-quarter benefit of the replacement-channel price increases taken in January 2025 and
expects input costs to stay broadly stable into the June quarter.

Capital expenditure for the quarter was INR 468.55 Crore, spent largely on the truck and
bus radial line at Chennai and on the captive renewable capacity commissioned during the
quarter. Full-year capital expenditure for FY25 was INR 1,742.30 Crore, within the range
guided at the start of the year, and the Board has approved a comparable outlay for FY26
to be met from internal accruals.

The above results were reviewed by the Audit Committee and approved by the Board of
Directors at its meeting held on 9 May 2025. The statutory auditors have carried out an
audit of these results and have issued an unmodified opinion.
