MANAGEMENT COMMENTARY
Farm tyre volumes were seasonally strong through the March quarter, helped by a healthy
rabi season and by tractor original equipment schedules that held firm to the end of the
financial year, while the passenger car replacement channel grew modestly on the wider
dealer network. Natural rubber prices eased from the elevated levels of the December
quarter and the raw material basket was the most favourable of the financial year, although
carbon black and synthetic rubber firmed again towards the end of March. The Company did
not change list prices during the quarter and expects the raw material basket to turn mildly
adverse in the June quarter.

Capital expenditure for the quarter was USD 3.82 Million, directed to a curing press line
for large farm sizes, to debottlenecking of the mixing area and to plant information
systems at Ballabgarh. Capital expenditure for the full financial year was USD 13.40
Million and was met entirely from internal accruals.

The audited financial results were reviewed by the Audit Committee and approved by the
Board of Directors at its meeting held on 9 May 2025. The statutory auditors have issued an
unmodified opinion on these results.
