MANAGEMENT COMMENTARY
Replacement demand firmed up through the March quarter as the trade restocked ahead of the
price revision taken in February, and the channel accounted for a larger share of the mix
than in the preceding quarter. Two-wheeler original equipment offtake was steadier rather
than stronger, with build schedules moderating after the festive quarter, while exports
held flat against a soft African and South-East Asian market. On the cost side, natural
rubber prices stayed well above the year-ago level for a second consecutive quarter and
carbon black and synthetic rubber were broadly stable sequentially; the February price
revision and a richer replacement mix recovered part of that, leaving the EBITDA margin a
little above the December quarter.

Capital expenditure for the quarter was Rs. 44.85 Crore, directed mainly at the two-wheeler
radial line at Madurai, the curing press upgrade and the captive solar tie-up commissioned
during the quarter. The Company incurred approximately Rs. 168 Crore of capital expenditure
in FY25, funded from internal accruals.

The audited financial results were audited by the Statutory Auditors, reviewed by the Audit
Committee and approved by the Board of Directors at its meeting held on 6 May 2025. The
figures for the quarter ended 31 March 2025 are the balancing figures between the audited
figures for the full financial year and the published year-to-date figures up to the third
quarter, which were subjected to a limited review.
