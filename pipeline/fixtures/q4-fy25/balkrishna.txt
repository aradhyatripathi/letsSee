MANAGEMENT COMMENTARY
Volumes for the quarter were 79,148 tonnes, up 9.2% year on year, the highest quarterly
despatch the Company has recorded, helped by restocking at European distributors ahead of
the spring season and by a firmer order book in the all-terrain and mining categories in
the Americas. Natural rubber prices eased through the quarter from the peaks seen in the
December quarter, and the Company drew down higher-cost inventory carried into January, so
the blended raw-material cost per tonne fell sequentially; carbon black and synthetic
rubber were broadly flat. Freight rates on the Europe lane also softened, which the
Company has retained rather than passed on. Realisations declined marginally on a richer
mix of larger-rim agricultural sizes carrying lower per-kilogram pricing.

Capital expenditure for the quarter was INR 388.40 Crore, spent principally on the Bhuj
mixing and curing lines, on the first phase of the carbon black plant, and on the captive
solar and wind capacity being added at Bhuj and Waluj. Capital expenditure for FY25 as a
whole was within the INR 1,400 Crore to INR 1,600 Crore range indicated at the start of
the year and was met entirely from internal accruals.

The audited financial results were reviewed by the Audit Committee and approved by the
Board of Directors at its meeting held on 15 May 2025. The statutory auditors have issued
an unmodified audit opinion on these results.
