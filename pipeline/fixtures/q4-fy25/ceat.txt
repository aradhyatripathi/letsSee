MANAGEMENT COMMENTARY
Revenue rose 10.3% year on year and 2.8% sequentially, with volume growth in the truck
and bus radial replacement channel and a strong close to the year in original equipment
fitments for passenger vehicles and two-wheelers. Profitability contracted sharply against
the March 2024 quarter: natural rubber remained elevated for a fourth consecutive quarter
and averaged close to INR 195 per kg, while carbon black and synthetic rubber contracts
were repriced upward from January 2025, together adding roughly 260 basis points to the
material cost ratio for the quarter. A price increase taken across the replacement
portfolio in February 2025 recovered only part of that, and the Company has guided to a
lagged pass-through over the following two quarters.

Capital expenditure of INR 254.18 Crore in the quarter was spent on the Halol two-wheeler
radial line, on the completion of the Nagpur speciality tyre debottlenecking, and on
routine mould and automation replacement across plants. Full-year capital expenditure for
FY25 closed at INR 927 Crore, and the Board has approved an FY26 outlay of approximately
INR 1,000 Crore to be met from internal accruals.

These results were reviewed by the Audit Committee and approved by the Board of Directors
at its meeting held on 25 April 2025, and have been audited by the statutory auditors, who
have issued an unmodified opinion on them.
