MANAGEMENT COMMENTARY
Farm tyre demand improved through the quarter as the tractor original equipment build
schedule recovered, and the consumer replacement channel was stable. Natural rubber costs
remained the principal input headwind and were higher than the March quarter, while
crude-derivative inputs including carbon black and synthetic rubber rose modestly. The
Company has not taken a general price increase during the quarter and expects the raw
material basket to remain broadly at current levels through the September quarter.

Capital expenditure for the quarter was USD 3.15 Million, spent on farm tyre curing
capacity and on plant safety and environment projects at Ballabgarh. Planned capital
expenditure for the remainder of FY26 is approximately USD 12 Million and will be met from
internal accruals.

The unaudited financial results were reviewed by the Audit Committee and approved by the
